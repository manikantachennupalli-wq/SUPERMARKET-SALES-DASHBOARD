📌 Project Overview :
This project analyzes supermarket sales transaction data and presents the insights through
an interactive Excel/Power BI dashboard. It covers sales, profit, product performance,
category-wise revenue, and payment behavior across branches.

The dashboard is built as an interactive report with the following sections:

1. **Header / Title Bar** – "Sales Dashboard – Supermarket Shop" with company branding.

 **Filters (Slicers)**
   - *Sale Type*: Direct Sales, Online, Wholesaler
   - *Payment Mode*: Cash, Online
   - *Year*: 2021, 2022
   - *Month*: Jan–Dec

   -  **KPI Cards**
   - Total Sale ($15,263)
   - Total Profit ($2,502)
   - Profit % (16%)

   - **Top Performers**
   - Top Product (highest-selling product by quantity/revenue)
   - Top Category (highest revenue-generating category)

   - 5. **Monthly Trend Chart** – Combo chart showing Sale, Profit, and Profit % by month.

6. **Product Chart** – Horizontal bar chart ranking products by revenue.
   
8. **Sale Type Donut Chart** – Revenue split across Direct Sales, Online, Wholesaler.

9.  **Daily Trend Chart** – Area chart showing day-wise sales fluctuation across the month.

10.   **Payment Mode Donut Chart** – Revenue share between Cash and Online payments.

11.  **Category Treemap** – Visual comparison of revenue contribution by category (Catagory01–04).

12.   Purpose:
The dashboard enables quick, visual monitoring of:
- Overall sales and profitability trends
- Best-performing products and categories
- Preferred payment modes and sale channels
- Seasonal/monthly and daily sales patterns

 Tools Used:
- Excel / Power BI (for dashboard visualization)
- CSV data source for raw transactional records
